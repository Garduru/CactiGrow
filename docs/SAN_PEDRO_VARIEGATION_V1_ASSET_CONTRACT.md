## Scope and genetic truth

- Canonical species ID and `imageKey`: `echinopsis-pachanoi` (*Echinopsis pachanoi*, San Pedro). Canonical feature ID: `variegation`; display name **Variegation**, family `chlorophyll`, classification `mutation`, art tag `variegated` in `src/data/geneticFeatures.ts`.
- A `Plant` stores this in `geneticFeatures: GeneticFeatureState[]`, separate from its five quantitative ratings and `specialVariants`. A state has a genotype, `expressed`, `expressionStrength` (0–100), stability, and origin. The definition calls for variable expression, with a configured expressed-strength range of 35–100. The state is not a pigmentation map.
- Current sexual inheritance is a game-model recessive `allelic` pair (`feature`/`standard`). Each parent can transmit a feature allele; two feature alleles can pass the configured 0.9 expression roll, after which strength is resolved once. The spontaneous path may create two feature alleles. A carrier or unexpressed state must **never** display Variegation. Do not modify inheritance, expression, spontaneous odds, or eligibility to solve art limitations.
- No stored variegation percentage, sector geometry, side, stripe count, color allele, or image pattern exists. `expressionStrength` is a stored gameplay rating, **not** a literal painted coverage percentage. A chosen artwork family is bounded, deterministic presentation, not a new genotype or discovery fact.
- `cloneLineId` is stable genetic-line identity and should select the recognizable pattern family across stages and future vegetative copies. Physical specimen `id` may retain today's small pose difference but must not change the pattern family or place a mask independently. New sexual offspring receive their own clone line and may choose another pattern even when they inherit the feature.

## Locked production decision: authored pigmentation masks

Use the **approved body → directly authored, body-registered pigmentation mask → compatible surface/spine modules → any independently approved Shiny treatment → presentation context**. The approved Normal San Pedro artwork remains authoritative for silhouette, ribs, geometry, texture, shading, scars, painted spines, registration, and baseline. The mask changes pigmentation of cactus tissue only. The default asset is **not** a separately rendered Variegated cactus, an extracted image-difference plate, or an opaque replacement-color overlay.

The preferred mask is a transparent, body-registered intensity map: its alpha and/or grayscale/channel values define **where and how strongly** reduced-chlorophyll pigmentation applies. Empty pixels mean no treatment. It must include no duplicated green body, spines, scars, background, or copied lighting geometry. The shared recipe transforms/tints the underlying Normal epidermis within that authored mask toward restrained pale yellow-green while preserving the Normal body's highlights, shadows, rib depth, and painted texture. The deterministic Canvas implementation and React/Phaser parity have passed human runtime review for the currently supported masks; future treatments still require their own review.

If a pure strength mask cannot reach the approved quality, a **bounded transparent painted pigmentation plate** may add controlled color information while still leaving Normal texture, lighting, alpha, and geometry authoritative. Such a plate is not a mostly opaque replacement skin or full cactus image. A complete reviewed composite is an explicit exception only when a particular compatible body/stage combination cannot be represented cleanly with modules; it must not become the normal cross-product pipeline.

The Normal base currently includes painted spines and details. Author the mask directly against its exact epidermis, excluding areoles/spines and scars from the beginning. Do not tint every nontransparent pixel or reconstruct spines after a replacement render. A future Dense Spination module, if approved, belongs above pigmentation and must register to the selected body.

## Pattern continuity and deterministic selection

Mature Patterns A, B, and C plus Developing Pattern A and Juvenile Pattern A are human-approved, locked source masks. Pattern A is complete at three of four stages; Seedling A and Juvenile/Developing B/C remain unstarted. Each stage mask must be authored against its own approved body geometry; never mechanically shrink a later-stage mask. Avoid random splatter or a nominal family that cannot be distinguished in play.

The future shared resolver should select only among manifest-declared, complete, compatible four-stage families. Use a **versioned, salted clone-line hash channel** derived from the existing stable identity approach; do not reuse `formIndex % 3` as though three masks already exist. Preserve the selected family across growth-stage swaps, scene reloads, React rerenders, and future same-line clones. Each stage may have newly authored geometry following its own ribs and new growth, while preserving the family's recognizable side/sector flow. Physical specimen ID may contribute today's small approved rotation/scale presentation, applied to the **whole plant composite** after registration, never separately to the mask. No `Math.random()` during render. Adding or reordering families must not silently remap existing clone lines; future authoritative persistence may store a visual-version/family assignment if a manifest migration requires it.

**Pattern family and expression are independent axes.** Family answers **where** pigmentation appears; expression answers **how strongly or how much** it appears. Pattern B's greater measured Mature mask coverage than C does not make B a different expression class. Pattern C is not synonymous with full Variegation. A future shared resolver can select a stable family from clone-line identity independently of a bounded visual treatment selected from the stored `expressionStrength`. Do not map `35–100` directly to a coverage percentage or raw opacity, or author a complete asset set for every rating. The existing `GeneticFeatureState.expressionStrength` (0–100; configured expressed range 35–100 for Variegation) can likely support art-reviewed expression bands later, but exact thresholds and rendering behavior are not locked. Do not add genes, a full-variegation mutation ID, a coverage-percentage gene, or a graft-only genotype. Do not infer pattern family from hidden carrier state.

## Art direction and compositing

Use CactiGrow's stylized hand-painted 3D, clean semi-cartoon forms, medium detail, warm upper-left desert light, restrained texture noise, and mobile-readable silhouette. Pigmentation should look like reduced/patterned chlorophyll within cactus tissue: restrained warm cream, pale yellow-green, muted yellow-green, and pale natural green alongside the approved blue-/warm-green skin. Follow rib curvature and existing shading; retain readable valleys and areoles. Avoid neon/fluorescent yellow, emissive color, pure-white clipping, camouflage noise, or arbitrary paint splashes. **Variegation is not luminous.** All plant visual assets must contain no glow, halo, aura, bloom, surrounding light spill, baked background, or cast shadow unless a later effect is explicitly approved.

Intended **future shared recipe**, not current behavior:

1. Resolve species, visual stage, expressed feature IDs, clone-line identity, and stored Shiny. Select one approved body family/stage; Normal San Pedro uses the four unchanged production bodies.
2. Apply approved bounded Normal-body variation to the coherent body. Choose a registered pigmentation family for an **expressed** Variegation state and compatible body/stage. Use the authored mask to transform **underlying epidermis color** in local coordinates while preserving the Normal body's lighting, texture, and alpha. A richer plate is permitted only after explicit quality review.
3. Retain Normal painted spines; later add only compatible Dense Spination/surface plates above pigmentation at authored areoles. Apply any independently approved Shiny final treatment without washing out Variegation. Neither Dense Spination nor Shiny is implemented by this contract.
4. Apply the whole-composite pose/scale around the shared bottom-center anchor. Garden roots the plant directly in its soil; Nursery owns its front-facing pot and base occlusion (no visible soil or shadow in the approved temporary presentation); Plant Details uses a neutral preview. No pot, soil, bench, world shadow, or scene decoration enters the canonical plant descriptor or pigmentation mask.

`createPlantVisualDescriptor` retains the approved Normal body, resolves an explicitly requested compatible pattern through the shared manifest, and reports resolved and missing layers. React and Phaser use the same descriptor and approved `yellow-green-v02` compositor. Phaser preloads registered masks and caches generated canvas textures; React caches generated compositions. A missing mask leaves the compatible Normal body visible with PARTIAL support rather than claiming the requested pigmentation is rendered.

## Body compatibility and support states

Register each mask explicitly by `speciesVisualKey + bodyFamily + stage + pigmentationFeature + patternFamily + version`. A Normal-body mask is **not** universal. Crested, Monstrose, Melted, Spiral, and Dichotomous bodies each need a reviewed compatible mask family, or a deliberately approved composite/visual-dominance recipe. Never stretch a Normal mask onto a crest or irregular silhouette. Multiple structural features require an explicit combined body or visual-dominance fallback; gameplay features remain present regardless of art. Body family, epidermis region, baseline, and spine/areole landmarks are compatibility metadata, not genetic fields.

Use the Visual Genetics Matrix meanings: **FULL** only when all expressed visual roles (and Shiny if present) are rendered with approved compatible assets/effects; **PARTIAL** when a coherent approved body and at least one but not all expressed roles are shown; **FALLBACK** when only a code-drawn silhouette can be shown because compatible species/stage body art is missing. Normal San Pedro with no expressed visual features is FULL. Seedling A, Juvenile A, Developing A, and Mature A/B/C Variegation are FULL when no other requested visual role is unsupported. Seedling B/C, Juvenile B/C, and Developing B/C are PARTIAL: the approved compatible Normal body remains visible and the missing pigmentation role is reported. Preserve all feature truth in state and Plant Details, and expose missing-role reasons in development diagnostics rather than claiming visual completeness.
