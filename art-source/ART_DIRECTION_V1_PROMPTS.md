# Art Direction V1 generation record

New raster masters were generated with the built-in imagegen tool for this staging milestone. The existing locked Nursery V2 and Shop V1 runtime images were style references only; their bytes and registrations were not edited.

Runtime exports crop, register and downsample these masters. Generated alpha is preserved. Source masters are kept outside public runtime assets. Human final-art acceptance is pending.

## ground

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: reusable game ground terrain atlas for CactiGrow. Create a square 1024x1024 bitmap with EXACTLY 4 equal columns and 4 equal rows; each of the 16 cells is a separate seamless top-down 256x256 ground material, no gutters, no grid lines, no text, no borders. Match attached reference buildings' warm stylized hand-painted 3D semi-cartoon Southwestern desert materials, medium restrained detail, clean readable forms, soft late-morning sun from upper left; no pixel-art, photorealism, glossy surfaces, or scene composition. Row1: four subtle variants of light sandstone sand, muted warm sandy tan, quiet grains and at most two tiny pebbles per tile, no dunes. Row2: four subtle variants of packed earth, warm desaturated brown ochre, flat traversable smooth dirt with faint brushy dry earth flecks. Row3: four subtle variants of roadway earth, lighter dusty compacted earth, very faint worn texture, NO footprints, ruts or central motif, full tile ground. Row4: four subtle variants of rocky desert soil, subdued terracotta earth, sparse SMALL flat gravel. All four variants in each row have precisely the same neutral edge colors so repeats tile without visible seam. Subdued contrast; scenery and people need to remain readable. Flat ground camera overhead, no perspective square slabs. This is a utilitarian material sheet, not a decorative collage. Fill entire square.
